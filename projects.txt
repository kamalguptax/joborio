2. Product Vision & Problem Statement
Every industry has the same distribution problem: there are millions of job openings and thousands of courses, but reaching the right candidate through traditional job boards and Google Ads is expensive, competitive, and increasingly inefficient.
Meanwhile, hundreds of thousands of niche creators — Telegram channel owners, WhatsApp group admins, Instagram career pages, YouTube educators, newsletter writers, Substack authors, LinkedIn voices — already have the trust and attention of exactly the audiences employers and institutes want to reach. But they have no simple, scalable way to monetize that attention beyond one-off brand deals they have to negotiate manually.

The Platform Solves Both Sides
• For Creators (any niche, any country): One link, auto-filled with relevant jobs and courses, real-time earnings dashboard in USD, verified payouts — zero negotiation, zero collab management. A tech career YouTuber in Nigeria, a nursing-jobs Telegram admin in the Philippines, a finance Instagram page in the US, a government-exam coach in India — all use the same system.
• For Employers/Institutes (any industry, any market): Self-serve campaign launch, pay only on verified performance (click, registration, or application), reach niche audiences they cannot find on Google — without ever contacting or managing individual creators.
• For the Platform: A 25–30% cut of every transaction, sitting between supply and demand with tracking, trust, and payout infrastructure as the moat. USD-denominated operations simplify multi-country expansion.

Why USD-Only Payouts
Running a multi-currency payout system is operationally expensive and legally complex (FX risk, country-specific banking integrations, per-jurisdiction compliance). USD is the global default for creator/affiliate payouts — platforms like Fiverr, Upwork, Impact.com, and ShareASale all settle in USD. Creators worldwide are accustomed to receiving USD and converting locally. This simplifies:
• One ledger currency — no FX reconciliation.
• One payout rail — Wise (cheapest cross-border), PayPal (widest reach), or Payoneer (established in creator economy).
• One compliance surface — US-entity or India-entity paying out in USD, rather than managing local banking in 15+ countries.
• Creator trust — USD is a stable, universally understood denomination.

4. Pricing Models — Detailed Mechanics

4.1 PPC — Pay Per Click
Launch model — proven, lowest fraud risk, universal across all verticals.
• Trigger: A unique visitor clicks the creator’s tracked link and lands on the job/course destination page.
• Verification: Server-side click log records IP, device fingerprint, timestamp, referrer, and creator token. Dedup window: 1 click per IP+device+link per 10 minutes.
• Fraud filters: Datacenter/VPN IP blocklist, self-click detection, velocity cap (flag if hourly clicks > 5x creator’s 7-day avg).
• Payout flow: Click recorded → Pending (3–7 day hold) → Approved → Payable on next USD payout cycle.

4.2 PPR — Pay Per Registration
Higher value per event — used when the advertiser wants verified signups, not just traffic.
• Trigger: Visitor completes a registration form (job-portal signup, course enquiry, job-alert subscription) on the destination, confirmed via webhook or server-side postback.
• Verification: Email-verification gate (registered email must verify within 24 hrs). Duplicate check: one registration per email per program.
• Fraud filters: Disposable-email blocklist, rate limit (max 20 registrations/creator/hour), phone dedup where available.
• Payout flow: Registration event → Pending (email verification, 24 hrs) → Verified → Approved → Payable.
• Example: An online MBA platform runs a PPR campaign at $0.X/verified signup. A LinkedIn finance influencer (30K followers, global audience) promotes it in 3 posts. 220 signups, 190 email-verified. Gross = $X. Platform takes 30% , creator earns 70% USD.

4.3 PPA — Pay Per Application ( Second Phase )
Highest value, highest trust requirement — launch after PPC and PPR are stable.
• Trigger: Candidate submits a job application or course enrollment on the employer/institute’s own site, confirmed back to the platform.
• Verification: Employer/institute marks application as received (webhook or manual confirmation). Attribution window: 30 days from last creator-link click.
• Fraud filters: All PPC/PPR filters, plus application-quality scoring (if employer marks >30% from one creator as ‘irrelevant’, creator’s PPA eligibility reviewed).
• Payout flow: Application event → Pending (employer confirmation, up to 7 days) → Confirmed → Approved → Payable.
• Example: A global tech company runs a PPA campaign for senior developers at $X/application. A coding-tutorial YouTube channel (200K subs) promotes it. 85 applications, employer confirms 72 valid. Gross = $XA. Platform takes 30% ($XC), creator earns $XB USD.
 
5. Module-by-Module Feature Detail

Module A — Creator Signup & Onboarding
Purpose: Get a creator from zero to a live, sharable link in under 2 minutes — regardless of country or niche.
• Email + Google OAuth signup (global coverage, no platform-specific dependency).
• Handle/username claiming — creator picks slug, system checks availability, generates brand.link/@handle instantly.
• Niche/category selection: Technology, Healthcare, Finance & Accounting, Education & Upskilling, Sales & Marketing, Logistics & Supply Chain, Government & Public Sector, Skilled Trades, Creative & Design, Legal, Energy, Hospitality — extensible taxonomy.
• Country/region selection (for audience-matching, not gating — a creator in Nigeria can promote US jobs if their audience is US-based).
• Audience-size self-declaration (optional, feeds match-quality scoring).
• Onboarding checklist: profile photo → bio → niche → country → first share = ‘link is live’ confirmation.
Example: A Substack writer covering fintech careers in Southeast Asia signs up, picks @fintechjobsasia, selects Finance + Technology, sets audience region to Singapore/Malaysia/Indonesia, and has a live link in 90 seconds.

Module B — Public Creator Page & My Link
Purpose: The one link a creator shares everywhere — personalized, mobile-first, industry-agnostic.
• Creator branding: photo/logo, name, bio, social links.
• Auto-populated job/course feed based on creator’s niche + audience geography — refreshed every 2 hours.
• Job/course cards: title, company/institute, location (or ‘Remote’), salary/fee range (where available), CTA button.
• Search and filters for audience (location, category, experience level, remote/onsite).
• Share buttons: WhatsApp (one-tap), copy-link, native share, Telegram share.
• SEO-friendly: server-rendered, Open Graph tags, social preview image auto-generated.
• Page customization: color accent, pinned listings, hide specific items.
Example: A visitor in Germany clicks a link shared in a data-science Telegram group. They land on a clean mobile page showing the creator’s photo, bio (‘Helping data professionals find remote roles worldwide’), and 20 relevant job cards across US, UK, and Germany — no login required.

Module C — Job & Course Management
Purpose: Populate the platform with relevant, fresh inventory across all industries and geographies.
• Job-data ingestion from global aggregators (API) — automated, scheduled every 2 hours.
• Direct employer/institute submissions via the Program Marketplace (Module G).
• Auto-categorization by industry, role type, location, experience level, remote/onsite.
• Course categorization: domain, format (online/offline/hybrid), price (free/paid), certification (yes/no).
• Status management: Active, Paused, Expired, Budget Exhausted.
• Creator-job matching: niche + audience geography + audience interests scored against every active listing.
Example: 500 new tech jobs across US, UK, India, and UAE are ingested from Jooble at 6 AM UTC. By 6:05 AM, every creator whose niche includes ‘Technology’ and whose audience overlaps those countries has fresh listings on their page.

Module D — Tracking & Attribution Engine
Purpose: The single most critical module — every dollar of revenue flows through this system.
• Unique tracking token per creator+listing combination — embedded in every outbound link.
• Server-side event capture (not client-side JS — harder to spoof, works even if visitor blocks scripts).
• Event types: PageView, JobView, Click (PPC), Registration (PPR), Application (PPA).
• Full attribution chain: Creator → Campaign/Post (if UTM-tagged) → Job/Course → Event → Timestamp → IP + Device Fingerprint → Country (GeoIP).
• Deduplication: one countable event per IP+device+link per configurable time window.
• Self-click detection: creator’s own registered IP/device excluded.
• Last-touch attribution with configurable window (default 30 days for PPA).
Example: Creator A (UK) shares Job X. Candidate in London clicks at 10:14 AM GMT from an iPhone on Vodafone. System records: creator_id=A, job_id=X, event=click, ip=xxx, device_hash=yyy, referrer=t.me, geo=GB, timestamp=10:14. A second click from same IP+device at 10:18 is logged but not counted.

Module E — Bot & Fake Traffic Detection
Purpose: Protect unit economics and advertiser trust globally.
• Layer 1 — Free rule-based filters (launch): IP dedup, global datacenter/VPN blocklist (IP2Location LITE, free), self-click exclusion, referrer validation, velocity cap (flag if hourly clicks > 5x creator’s 7-day avg).
• Layer 2 — Manual review queue: flagged events go to admin queue, not auto-rejected. At early volume, 30 min/day is cheaper and more accurate than any fraud SaaS.
• Layer 3 — Hold-before-pay: every earning sits in ‘Pending’ for 3–7 days before ‘Approved.’ Most fraud patterns surface in this window.
• Layer 4 — Paid fraud API (add when manual review stops scaling): dedicated click-fraud service, priced per event.
Example: Creator B generates 800 clicks in 10 minutes from 3 IPs in an AWS datacenter range. Layer 1 flags 780. Layer 2 queues the remaining 20. Admin suspends Creator B’s pending earnings.

Module F — Creator Dashboard & Earnings (USD)
Purpose: Give creators worldwide real-time visibility into performance and earnings — all denominated in USD.
• Real-time metrics: page views, job/course views, clicks, registrations, applications (by event type).
• Earnings breakdown: PPC earnings, PPR earnings, PPA earnings, referral overrides — all in USD.
• Earnings states: Pending (held for verification), Approved (verified, awaiting payout), Paid (disbursed via Wise/PayPal/Payoneer).
• Top-performing listings: which jobs/courses generated the most clicks/earnings.
• Period comparison: this week vs last week, this month vs last month.
• Payout history: every disbursement with date, USD amount, transaction reference, payout method.
• Quick-share actions: one-tap share to WhatsApp, Telegram, copy link, generate post (if AI studio is enabled).

Module G — Program Marketplace (Employer/Institute Portal)
Purpose: Let employers and institutes from any country launch USD-denominated performance campaigns.
• Employer/institute signup and basic verification (business email, company URL).
• Program creation wizard: Title → Description → Pricing model (PPC/PPR/PPA) → Rate per event (USD) → Total budget (USD) → Target audience criteria (niche, geography, language, follower tier).
• Budget pre-funding in USD: employer deposits via Stripe/PayPal before program goes live — protects creators from non-payment.
• Anonymized match preview: ‘14 creators matched, avg audience-fit 88%’ — never names, handles, or contact details.
• Program dashboard: budget spent, remaining, impressions, clicks, registrations, applications, cost per event, anonymized creator-level performance.
• Pause/resume/extend controls.
Example: A UK edtech company launches a PPR campaign for their cybersecurity bootcamp at $0.60/verified signup, budget $3,000. System matches to 11 creators in Tech + Education niches with UK/US/India audiences. Within one week, 280 verified signups come in.

Module H — Auto-Matching Engine
Purpose: Replace manual outreach with algorithmic matching — works across every niche and geography.
• Scoring factors: creator niche vs program category, creator audience geography vs program target geography, creator trust-tier, creator historical conversion rate.
• Matched creators see the program in their ‘Available Campaigns’ feed — opt in with one tap.
• Employer sees aggregated match quality, never individual creator profiles.
• Auto-reallocation: if a campaign underperforms with one creator segment, remaining budget shifts toward better-converting matches.
Example: A global staffing firm posts 500 roles across healthcare (US, UK, UAE) at $0.05/click, budget $5,000. System scores 40 creators, shows program to top 25. 19 opt in. After 5 days, 6 creators drive 75% of valid clicks — budget auto-reallocates.

Module I — USD Payout System (Global)
Purpose: Reliable, scheduled, global creator payouts in USD — the trust foundation of the platform.
• Payout methods: Wise (lowest cross-border fees, ~0.5–1%), PayPal (widest global reach, ~2–3%), Payoneer (established in creator/freelancer economy). Creator chooses their preferred method.
• Minimum payout threshold: $10 USD (keeps transaction-fee ratio sane across all methods).
• Payout cycle: twice-monthly (1st and 15th), or on-demand once threshold is met.
• Payout states: Initiated → Processing → Completed / Failed (with retry).
• Creator KYC: identity verification + payout-method verification before first payout. Tax documentation (W-8BEN for non-US creators, W-9 for US creators) collected at onboarding if platform is US-entity.
• Admin manual-approval gate for first 3–6 months.
Example: Payout cycle runs on the 15th. System calculates: 280 creators have ‘Approved’ balances above $10, total $4,820. Admin reviews batch, approves. Wise fires 210 transfers, PayPal fires 55, Payoneer fires 15. 278 complete within 24 hrs, 2 fail (wrong details) — flagged for creator update.

Module J — Referral & Creator Growth Engine
Purpose: Creators recruit creators — the cheapest cross-border growth lever.
• Every creator gets a unique referral link.
• When referred creator generates first $10 in approved earnings, referrer earns a one-time bonus ($X) + will the ongoing 2–5% override on referred creator’s earnings for 2 months.
• Referral dashboard: invites sent, signups, active referrals, override earnings (USD).
Example: A Nigerian tech YouTuber shares their referral link in a creator community. 12 new creators sign up across Nigeria, Kenya, and South Africa. 7 become active. Referrer earns $35 in bonuses + ~$18/month in overrides.

Module K — AI Post Studio (Capped)  second Phase
Purpose: Help creators generate ready-to-share social content — language-aware, platform-optimized.
• Creator selects a listing → one tap generates: WhatsApp message, Instagram caption, LinkedIn post, Twitter/X post, short-form video script, hashtag suggestions.
• Language detection: generates content in the language the creator’s audience primarily uses.
• Every generated piece includes the creator’s tracked link automatically.
• Hard cap: 5 AI generations per creator per month (free tier). Non-negotiable at launch.
• Premium tier (future): higher cap, paid by creator or unlocked at higher trust-tier.
Example: A Spanish-speaking career coach selects a remote marketing role. System generates an Instagram caption in Spanish with relevant hashtags and the tracked link. Creator copies, posts, done in 15 seconds.

Module L — Admin Panel
Purpose: Centralized global operations.
• Creator management: approve, suspend, review performance, trust-tier assignment, country filter.
• Program/campaign management: approve, pause, close employer programs, country/niche filter.
• Job/course management: sources, categories, status, multi-country inventory health.
• Financial overview: total revenue (USD), total creator payouts (USD), platform margin, outstanding payables, by-country breakdown.
• Fraud queue: flagged events, investigation tools, earnings suspension/reversal.
• Payout batch review and approval (by payout method: Wise/PayPal/Payoneer).
• CMS: banners, announcements, FAQs, static pages, localized content.
• Support ticket queue.

Module M — Trust-Tier System
Purpose: Gate high-value models behind proven performance — same tiers worldwide.
• Nano (new): PPC only, manual payout review, standard fraud hold. We will automate after running 3 month . 
• Micro (after 1,000 verified clicks + 30 days active): PPC + PPR unlocked, fraud hold reduced to 3 days.
• Mid (after 5,000 verified clicks + 2 months + <5% invalid rate): PPC + PPR + PPA unlocked, auto-payout eligible.
• Macro (top 5% by volume + quality): all models, priority matching for premium campaigns, leaderboard featured.

Purpose: Structured, fair resolution for PPA/PPR disputes.

Purpose: Operate seamlessly across countries without rebuilding.

• Country-specific job/course inventory: aggregator feeds filtered by geography, shown to creators whose audience matches.
• Timezone-aware dashboards and payout schedules.
• Compliance: GDPR cookie consent for EU traffic, privacy-policy links per jurisdiction.
